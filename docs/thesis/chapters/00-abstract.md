# 摘要

随着云原生应用规模持续增长，头部云服务商的 Kubernetes 集群已普遍达到万节点乃至数万节点规模，调度器需同时具备承接海量 Pod 的高吞吐能力与不因单点失效而中断服务的高可用性。原生 kube-scheduler 采用单实例串行架构，调度吞吐存在明确上限、延迟随集群规模线性抬升；Leader Election 单活跃部署又使集群面临单点故障风险——吞吐见顶与单点故障共同构成亟待突破的可扩展性瓶颈。为此业界向多实例分布式架构演进；字节跳动开源的 Gödel Scheduler 通过 Dispatcher/Scheduler/Binder 三层解耦与节点分区机制实现并发调度，是目前最具代表性的开源分布式实现，但其独立部署的 Binder 在超高并发下重新引入绑定串行化瓶颈与跨进程通信开销，且节点分区归属动态漂移场景下的严格一致性保证仍缺乏完整论证。

针对以上问题，本文提出 ENO 架构与配套的分层绑定容错机制。架构上，ENO 采用"单 Dispatcher、多独立 Scheduler"的分发-执行解耦模型，将 Binder 执行链路与 Scheduler 决策实例同域化，消除跨组件事件传递与序列化开销；并通过 CacheAdapter 共享缓存适配层，使 Scheduler 与进程内 Binder 共享同一份 SchedulerCache 实例，实现零拷贝状态复用。机制上，本文基于 etcd 原子性语义构建覆盖 Bind 前后完整链路的四层结构化容错，并给出核心不变量"任一 Pod 至多绑定到一个节点"的完整证明要点。

实验验证与性能评估在 KWOK 仿真集群上进行，覆盖 100 至 10000 节点、7 类负载与 1/3 实例配置的 16 个对比场景，与 Gödel、kube-scheduler、Volcano、Koordinator 四类基线对照。正确性方面，全部 144 次 run 的绑定成功率均为 100%，核心不变量 apiserver 断言全部通过，Layer 0 与 Layer 3 的故障注入专项各六项判据全部达标。性能方面，以总工作量除以总完成时间作为有效吞吐口径：单实例下 ENO 较 kube-scheduler 高 1.8%~22.5%，较 Volcano、Koordinator 分别高约 6 倍与 12 倍以上；16 个场景中 14 个高于 Gödel，平均提升约 12%；主评估场景 s3/w3 的 P99 调度延迟由 32.57 s 降至 16.22 s；实例数由 1 增至 3 时 ENO 有效吞吐提升 17.5% 而 Gödel 仅 1.4%。上述结果表明本文所提架构与容错机制在超大规模场景下同时具备性能优势与一致性保证。

关键词：Kubernetes 调度器，分布式调度，etcd 一致性，绑定容错，进程内绑定，大规模集群

# Abstract

As cloud-native workloads continue to grow, Kubernetes clusters at leading cloud providers now routinely reach tens of thousands of nodes, demanding both high throughput to sustain massive Pod scheduling and high availability against single-point failures. The native kube-scheduler adopts a single-instance serial architecture whose throughput has a definite upper bound and whose latency rises linearly with cluster size; its Leader-Election single-active deployment further exposes the cluster to single-point-of-failure risk — throughput saturation and single-point failure together form the scalability bottleneck. In response, the community has evolved toward multi-instance distributed architectures; ByteDance's open-source Gödel Scheduler achieves concurrent scheduling through a Dispatcher/Scheduler/Binder three-layer decoupling and node-partition mechanism and stands as the most representative open-source distributed implementation, but its independently deployed Binder reintroduces a binding-phase serialization bottleneck and cross-process communication overhead under very-high concurrency, and strict consistency guarantees under runtime drift of node-partition ownership still lack a complete analysis.

To address these problems, this thesis proposes the ENO architecture with a companion layered binding fault-tolerance mechanism. Architecturally, ENO adopts a "single-Dispatcher, multi-independent-Scheduler" dispatch–execution-decoupled model that co-locates the Binder's execution path with the Scheduler decision instance to eliminate cross-component event passing and serialization overhead; a CacheAdapter shared-cache adaptation layer further lets the Scheduler and the in-process Binder share the same SchedulerCache instance for zero-copy state reuse. Mechanism-wise, based on etcd's atomic semantics, this thesis constructs a four-layer structured fault-tolerance chain covering the full path around Bind, and provides a complete proof outline for the core invariant "each Pod is bound to at most one node".

Experimental verification and performance evaluation are carried out on KWOK-simulated clusters covering 100 to 10,000 nodes, seven workload classes, and two instance configurations (1 and 3 instances), for 16 comparison scenarios against Gödel, kube-scheduler, Volcano, and Koordinator. For correctness, the binding success rate stays at 100% across all 144 runs, the apiserver-side invariant assertion passes on every run, and the six-criterion Layer 0 and Layer 3 fault-injection suites are each fully satisfied. For performance, using total workload divided by total completion time as the effective-throughput metric: in the single-instance case, ENO is 1.8%–22.5% higher than kube-scheduler and roughly 6× and 12× higher than Volcano and Koordinator respectively; ENO exceeds Gödel in 14 of the 16 scenarios with an average improvement of about 12%; the primary scenario s3/w3 sees its P99 scheduling latency drop from 32.57 s to 16.22 s; and from 1 to 3 instances, ENO's effective throughput improves by 17.5% versus Gödel's 1.4%. These results confirm that the proposed architecture and fault-tolerance mechanism deliver both performance gains and consistency guarantees at very-large scale.

Keywords: Kubernetes scheduler; distributed scheduling; etcd consistency; binding fault tolerance; in-process binding; large-scale cluster
