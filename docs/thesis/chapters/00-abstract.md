# 摘要

随着云原生应用规模的持续增长，头部互联网与云服务商的 Kubernetes 集群已普遍达到万节点乃至数万节点的规模，业务对调度器提出了双重要求：秒级承接海量 Pod 的高吞吐能力，以及长期运行中不因单点失效而中断服务的高可用性。原生 kube-scheduler 采用单实例串行架构，其调度吞吐存在明确上限、延迟随集群规模线性抬升；Leader Election 单活跃实例的部署形态又使集群面临单点故障风险——吞吐见顶与单点故障共同构成亟待突破的可扩展性瓶颈。为突破上述瓶颈，业界向多实例分布式架构演进；字节跳动开源的 Gödel Scheduler 通过 Dispatcher/Scheduler/Binder 三层解耦与节点分区机制实现了多实例并发调度，是目前最具代表性的开源分布式实现。然而，其独立部署的 Binder 组件在超高并发场景下重新引入了绑定阶段的串行化瓶颈与跨进程通信开销；同时，节点分区归属在运行时动态漂移场景下的严格一致性保证仍缺乏完整论证。

针对以上问题，本文提出 ENO 架构与配套的分层绑定容错机制。架构上，ENO 采用"单 Dispatcher、多独立 Scheduler"的分发-执行解耦模型，将 Binder 绑定执行链路与 Scheduler 决策实例同域化，消除跨组件的事件传递与序列化开销；并通过 CacheAdapter 共享缓存适配层，使 Scheduler 与进程内 Binder 通过对象引用共享同一份 SchedulerCache 内存实例，实现零拷贝的状态复用。机制上，本文基于 etcd 提供的原子性语义，构建覆盖 Bind 前后完整链路的四层结构化容错，并给出核心不变量"任一 Pod 至多绑定到一个节点"的完整证明要点，明确每一层如何依赖 etcd 语义维护该不变量。

评估在 KWOK 仿真集群上进行，覆盖 100 至 10000 节点、7 类工作负载与 1/3 两种实例配置的 16 个对比场景，与 Gödel、kube-scheduler、Volcano、Koordinator 四类基线对照。吞吐采用与实验时长无关的有效吞吐口径（总工作量除以总完成时间）。结果显示：单实例场景下，ENO 有效吞吐较 kube-scheduler 高 1.8%~22.5%，较 Volcano、Koordinator 分别高约 6 倍与 12 倍以上；ENO 在 16 个场景中 14 个高于 Gödel，平均提升约 12%；主评估场景 s3/w3 的 P99 调度延迟由 32.57 s 降至 16.22 s；当实例数由 1 增至 3 时，ENO 有效吞吐提升 17.5%，而 Gödel 仅提升 1.4%。上述结果表明本文所提架构与容错机制在超大规模场景下同时具备性能优势与一致性保证。

关键词：Kubernetes 调度器，分布式调度，etcd 一致性，绑定容错，进程内绑定，大规模集群

# Abstract

As cloud-native applications continue to grow, production Kubernetes clusters at leading internet companies and cloud providers now routinely reach tens of thousands of nodes, placing a two-fold demand on the scheduler: enough throughput to sustain massive Pod-scheduling requests at second-level granularity, and enough availability to keep the cluster schedulable in the face of single-point failures. The native kube-scheduler adopts a single-instance serial architecture whose scheduling throughput has a definite upper bound and whose latency rises linearly with cluster size; its Leader-Election-based single-active deployment further exposes the cluster to single-point-of-failure risk — throughput saturation and single-point failure together form the scalability bottleneck that must be broken through. To break through this bottleneck, the community has evolved toward multi-instance distributed architectures; ByteDance's open-source Gödel Scheduler achieves concurrent multi-instance scheduling through a Dispatcher/Scheduler/Binder three-layer decoupling and node-partition mechanism, and stands as the most representative open-source distributed implementation to date. However, its independently deployed Binder reintroduces a binding-phase serialization bottleneck and cross-process communication overhead under very-high concurrency; meanwhile, strict consistency guarantees under runtime dynamic drift of node-partition ownership still lack a complete analysis.

To address these problems, this thesis proposes the ENO architecture together with a companion layered binding fault-tolerance mechanism. Architecturally, ENO adopts a "single-Dispatcher, multi-independent-Scheduler" dispatch–execution-decoupled model, co-locating the Binder's binding execution path with the Scheduler decision instance to eliminate cross-component event passing and serialization overhead; a CacheAdapter shared-cache adaptation layer additionally allows the Scheduler and the in-process Binder to share the same SchedulerCache memory instance by object reference, achieving zero-copy state reuse. Mechanism-wise, based on the atomic semantics provided by etcd, this thesis constructs a four-layer structured fault-tolerance chain covering the full path around Bind, and provides a complete proof outline for the core invariant "each Pod is bound to at most one node", explicitly showing how each layer preserves this invariant through etcd semantics.

Evaluation is carried out on KWOK-simulated clusters covering 100 to 10,000 nodes, seven workload classes, and two instance configurations (1 and 3 instances), for a total of 16 comparison scenarios against Gödel, kube-scheduler, Volcano, and Koordinator. Throughput is measured under the duration-independent effective-throughput convention — total workload divided by total completion time. Results show that in the single-instance case, ENO's effective throughput is 1.8%–22.5% higher than kube-scheduler and roughly 6× and 12× higher than Volcano and Koordinator, respectively; ENO exceeds Gödel in 14 of the 16 scenarios with an average improvement of about 12%; in the primary scenario s3/w3, P99 scheduling latency drops from 32.57 s to 16.22 s; and when the instance count rises from 1 to 3, ENO's effective throughput improves by 17.5% while Gödel's improves by only 1.4%. These results confirm that the proposed architecture and fault-tolerance mechanism deliver both performance gains and consistency guarantees at very-large scale.

Keywords: Kubernetes scheduler; distributed scheduling; etcd consistency; binding fault tolerance; in-process binding; large-scale cluster
